**Junteng Liu** is a first-year Ph.D. candidate in the [HKUST NLP Group](https://hknlp.github.io/) at The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), where he is supervised by [Professor Junxian He](https://www.junxian.ai/). He received his B.Eng. in 2024 from Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU), where he was also advised by Professor Junxian He.

His research focuses on **natural language processing (NLP)** and **machine learning (ML)**. His current research interests include:

- LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning
- Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLM)
- LLM Truthfulness and Interpretability

## Education
